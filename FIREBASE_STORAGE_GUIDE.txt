================================================================
   FIREBASE STORAGE SETUP — FREE TIER GUIDE
================================================================

To enable image uploads for SaraWorld, Firebase requires the
"Blaze" plan. Don't worry — IT IS FREE for our usage level.

================================================================
WHY IT ASKS FOR AN UPGRADE
================================================================

Firebase changed their policy — Storage now requires the Blaze
(pay-as-you-go) plan. But "pay-as-you-go" does NOT mean you
will be charged. You only pay IF you exceed the free limits,
which a small jewelry website will NEVER reach.

Think of it like a prepaid SIM — you add a card but only pay
if you use extra data beyond your free quota.

================================================================
HOW TO UPGRADE (takes 2 minutes)
================================================================

1. Go to Firebase Console → your project (saraworld)
2. Click "Upgrade" at the bottom-left
3. Select "Blaze" plan
4. Add a debit/credit card
5. You will see: "Estimated monthly cost: $0.00"
6. Click "Purchase"
7. Now go to Build → Storage → Get Started → Test Mode → Done

That's it. Your card will NOT be charged.

================================================================
WILL WE EVER EXCEED THIS?
================================================================

NO. Here's why:

- A jewelry showcase with 200-500 items uses ~500 MB max
  (Free limit: 5,000 MB = 5 GB)

- Even if 500 people visit per day, downloads would be
  ~300 MB/day (Free limit: 1,000 MB = 1 GB/day)

- Admin uploads maybe 5-10 images per week
  (Free limit: 20,000 uploads/day)

This site could run 100% FREE for YEARS without hitting
any limit.

================================================================
HOW LONG DOES THE FREE TIER LAST?
================================================================

- There is NO time limit or trial period
- The free limits are PERMANENT — they don't expire
- You get these limits every single day/month, forever
- Google has maintained these free limits since 2016
- If you stay within limits, your bill is $0 every month

================================================================
WHAT IF WE SOMEHOW EXCEED A LIMIT?
================================================================

- Firebase sends email warnings BEFORE you're charged
- You can set a budget alert (e.g. alert at $1)
- You can downgrade back to free "Spark" plan anytime
  (you just lose Storage access)
- Typical overage costs are tiny:
  Storage: $0.026/GB per month
  Downloads: $0.12/GB
